Description

Apartment villa Hvar Croatia embodies sophisticated living in the heart of Dalmatia’s most sought-after destination. Set within vibrant Hvar Town, this property offers 450 m² of contemporary space, spread across four levels and designed for refined comfort and flexibility.

Inside, the villa contains six bedrooms and six bathrooms, and includes five fully furnished, equipped apartments. Premium details define the living experience: high-quality Marazzi ceramic tiles, Shiko joinery, and an advanced VRV heating and cooling system with a heat pump. Underfloor heating lines every apartment, while a Fisher solar system ensures efficient hot water. External thermal insulation and security features, such as entrance doors, smart locks, electronically operated shutters, and outdoor video surveillance, provide peace of mind and ease.

With a 488 m² plot and a private swimming pool, the property is both an investment opportunity and an inviting coastal holiday retreat. This apartment villa Hvar Croatia offers modern, energy-efficient comfort in an exceptional location, appealing to those who value quality, security, and architectural integrity.
