Operational team

Behind the leading boutique luxury brokers

Estoria & Co was founded with one goal: to elevate the way exclusive properties are presented and sold, both nationally and internationally.

A compact, specialized team forms the core of Estoria & Co, complemented by a network of independent experts in luxury real estate, sales, marketing, and internet technology. We strongly believe in boutique agencies that operate and collaborate as family-run businesses, because their personal and content-driven approach has proven to be more effective than the volume-driven methods of franchise brokers. Thanks to this combination of vision, knowledge, and experience, we create a powerful luxury home platform that brings together buyers, sellers, their brokers, and developers in a targeted way.

Every collaboration and introduction is carefully tailored, with maximum attention to discretion, quality, and results. What should be self-evident in high-end real estate is what we deliver: personal guidance, a well-thought-out strategy, and flawless execution.

Estoria & Co continues to build on this model: strong in substance, driven by quality, and focused on clients who expect more from the way luxury properties, also across borders, are positioned, purchased, and sold.
