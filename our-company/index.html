Estoria & Co

Leading boutique brokers, redefining real estate practice

At Estoria & Co, we curate a unique portfolio of luxury residences from hundreds of family-owned boutique agencies specializing in prime real estate. Through our exclusive peer-to-peer international network, we connect discerning buyers with trusted boutique brokers. With a presence in more than 35 prime locations across over 20 countries, Estoria & Co showcases an unparalleled collection of numerous distinctive luxury homes.

Curated boutique brokers
You Can Trust

In a real estate world dominated by corporate chains, we believe in a more tailored, human approach. New franchises keep multiplying, yet they remain uniform and add little real value, while digital innovation has made their dominance increasingly irrelevant. Instead, true expertise, personal commitment, and a hands-on strategy define success in luxury real estate.
The leading boutique brokers presenting their listings at Estoria & Co are not anonymous agents, but curated boutique seller’s agencies, selected for their integrity, local knowledge, and proven track record in prime real estate. By bringing together this trusted circle of boutique real estate agencies, we ensure that every property showcased through Estoria & Co meets the highest standards of quality and authenticity.

The growing demand for
personal buyer’s advisors

Today’s affluent homebuyers expect more than just listings; they seek expert guidance from professionals who represent their best interests—objective, independent, and certified, supported by finely tuned networks within their regions.
That’s why we go beyond traditional real estate services, ensuring that buyers’ interests are no longer left in the hands of selling agents. Together with our buying support division, Estoria Property Finders & Advisors (estoriaproperty.com), we deliver a unique combination: an elite international luxury property platform paired with tailored, independent property advisory services.

Why choose a Personal Property Advisor

The real estate landscape has changed dramatically. While online platforms have made listings more accessible, they have also created confusion. Duplicate properties, outdated listings, and unreliable agents make it difficult to navigate the market efficiently.

Many international buyers prefer a personal, trusted advisor who represents only their interests - offering conflict-free guidance and exclusive access to the right properties.

At Estoria & Co, clients gain access to curated selections of prime luxury properties that precisely match their criteria; homes genuinely available at the time of inquiry. From personalized property searches and private viewings to expert negotiations and full transaction management, we handle every detail to make international home-buying seamless and stress-free. As a trusted name in luxury real estate, we provide privileged access to the latest listings from leading agencies as well as exclusive off-market properties.

Unlike traditional portals, Estoria & Co partners exclusively with vetted boutique brokers and offers certified, conflict-free buyer advisory services; ensuring discretion, expertise, and tailored support throughout your property journey.
