Privacy Policy


BAERZ & CO | INTERNATIONAL LUXURY REAL ESTATE

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

Baerz & Co respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

As an international alliance of carefully selected Boutique Luxury Brokers and independent Personal Property Advisors, we recognise the importance of discretion, confidentiality and responsible handling of personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal data when you visit www.baerz.com, submit a property enquiry, request professional assistance or communicate with us.

We do not sell personal information, trade in personal data or disclose personal details to unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

We process personal data in accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), applicable Dutch privacy legislation and other relevant legal requirements.

2. Who Is Responsible for Your Personal Data?

The organisation responsible for personal data collected and processed through Baerz.com is:

Baerz & Co B.V.

A private limited liability company established in the Netherlands.

Chamber of Commerce (KVK): 70912610

Statutory seat: Amsterdam, the Netherlands

Website: www.baerz.com

Email: info@baerz.com

Baerz & Co acts as the data controller where it determines the purposes and essential means of processing personal information.

Independent Boutique Luxury Brokers, Personal Property Advisors and other affiliated professionals remain responsible for complying with applicable data protection legislation in relation to their own professional activities.

Depending on their role and the circumstances, these professionals may act as independent data controllers or as processors acting under Baerz's instructions.

3. Personal Information We Collect

We collect only personal information reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide requested information, facilitate professional assistance and operate our Website.

3.1 Information You Provide

When contacting Baerz or submitting a property enquiry, you may provide:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number.
  • Your country of residence.
  • Your preferred property location.
  • Your property preferences and search criteria.
  • Your indicative purchasing budget, where voluntarily provided.
  • Information included in your messages or correspondence.

We do not routinely request identification documents, detailed financial records or other sensitive personal information for ordinary property enquiries.

Where additional information is required for a specific service or legal obligation, the relevant professional will be responsible for explaining the purpose and applicable privacy requirements.

3.2 Technical Website Information

When you visit Baerz.com, certain technical information may be processed, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Website language preferences.
  • Pages visited.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Technical error and security information.
  • Limited Website usage statistics.

This information is used for Website functionality, security, technical maintenance and permitted statistical analysis.

3.3 Correspondence and Enquiry Records

When you contact Baerz, relevant communications and follow-up activities may be recorded in our own Customer Relationship Management system (CRM).

These records may include enquiry details, property preferences, correspondence, relevant notes and the progress of a requested property search.

The purpose is to ensure professional, consistent and appropriate handling of enquiries.

3.4 Information Received from Other Sources

Where necessary for a requested service, we may receive relevant information from an independent Personal Property Advisor, Listing Partner or another professional involved in handling your enquiry.

Where required by law, we will provide appropriate information about the source and use of such personal data.

We do not purchase personal databases for unsolicited commercial marketing.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We process personal information only for specific and legitimate purposes, including:

Responding to enquiries

To answer questions about properties, developments or services available through the Baerz alliance.

Facilitating professional assistance

To connect users with an appropriate independent Personal Property Advisor or other relevant professional where requested or necessary for handling an enquiry.

Managing correspondence

To maintain relevant communication records and ensure appropriate follow-up.

Providing relevant property information

To communicate about properties or opportunities related to a user's expressed interests and ongoing enquiry.

Website operation and security

To operate, maintain, protect and improve the Website and its technical infrastructure.

Legal compliance

To comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Protection of legitimate interests

Where necessary and proportionate, to prevent misuse, resolve disputes or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not use personal information submitted through property enquiries for unrelated third-party advertising or commercial data trading.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Baerz processes personal data only where a valid legal basis under the GDPR exists.

Depending on the purpose and circumstances, these include:

Legitimate interests – Article 6(1)(f) GDPR

For responding to enquiries, managing correspondence and professional introductions, protecting our systems and conducting ordinary business activities, provided our legitimate interests are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of the individuals concerned.

Contractual necessity – Article 6(1)(b) GDPR

Where processing is necessary to take steps requested by an individual before entering into a contract with Baerz or to perform an agreement to which that individual is a party.

Legal obligations – Article 6(1)(c) GDPR

Where processing is required to comply with applicable legislation.

Consent – Article 6(1)(a) GDPR

Where consent is legally required, including for certain electronic marketing communications or optional technologies.

Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

Visiting the Website or submitting a property enquiry does not constitute blanket consent to unrelated processing or marketing activities.

6. Sharing Personal Information Within the Baerz Alliance

Baerz operates through an international alliance of independent Boutique Luxury Brokers and affiliated Personal Property Advisors.

We do not automatically distribute personal information among alliance members.

Where a user requests property search or acquisition assistance, relevant information may be made available to an appropriately selected independent Personal Property Advisor.

Only information reasonably necessary for handling the request will be shared.

Submitting a property enquiry does not automatically result in disclosure of the prospective buyer's full contact details to the selling broker or project developer.

Where necessary and lawful, limited identifying information may be used to coordinate an enquiry or determine whether a prospective buyer is already being assisted by a particular professional.

Additional information may be disclosed where necessary for the requested assistance and where a valid legal basis exists.

Independent professionals must comply with applicable data protection legislation and appropriate confidentiality requirements.

Their respective responsibilities as independent controllers or processors are determined by their actual activities and applicable law.

7. Technical Service Providers and AI Tools

Baerz operates its own CRM system and uses selected external technology services to support its activities.

These include:

TransIP

For Website hosting, technical infrastructure and email services.

Google Analytics

For limited statistical analysis of Website use and performance.

OpenAI / ChatGPT

As a supporting tool for internal administrative, editorial, communication and business-related activities.

Where AI-supported tools are used, personal information is minimised or excluded wherever reasonably possible.

Any processing of personal information through such tools must have a valid legal basis and be subject to appropriate safeguards.

Baerz does not intentionally submit personal information from property enquiries for unrelated AI model training.

Technical service providers processing personal information on behalf of Baerz are subject to appropriate contractual and data protection requirements.

8. International Data Processing and Transfers

Baerz & Co operates an international alliance of independent Boutique Luxury Brokers and Personal Property Advisors, including professionals established both within and outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Our primary Website hosting, email infrastructure and own CRM environment are maintained through our Netherlands-based technical infrastructure.

Where necessary to respond to enquiries or facilitate assistance requested by a user, relevant personal information may be made available to an appropriately selected independent professional located outside the EEA.

Such information is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the requested purpose.

International transfers of personal data are carried out in accordance with the applicable requirements of the GDPR.

Where a destination benefits from a European Commission adequacy decision, personal data may be transferred on that basis.

For transfers to other countries, Baerz applies the appropriate legal safeguards required for the transfer, including, where relevant, European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses and any necessary supplementary measures.

Certain external technology providers may also process information outside the EEA, subject to the applicable legal requirements and safeguards.

We do not sell personal information or transfer personal data internationally for unrelated third-party marketing purposes.

Further information about relevant international transfers and applicable safeguards may be requested at info@baerz.com.

9. Retention of Personal Information

Baerz retains personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or for as long as required by applicable law.

We apply the principles of data minimisation and storage limitation.

Retention depends on the purpose and nature of the information:

Property enquiries and correspondence

Information is retained while an enquiry remains active and thereafter only for as long as necessary for appropriate follow-up, applicable legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Property search and advisory communications

Information relating to an ongoing property search is retained for the duration necessary to handle that request and subsequently only where a continuing lawful purpose exists.

Newsletter subscriptions

Subscription information is retained while the subscription remains active. Following unsubscription, personal information is removed from active marketing lists, except for minimal information necessary to respect the opt-out or comply with legal obligations.

Technical and analytical information

Information is retained only for the operational or statistical purposes for which it is necessary, in accordance with applicable system settings and privacy requirements.

Legally required records

Where statutory retention periods apply, relevant information is retained for the period prescribed by law.

Personal information that is no longer necessary for a lawful purpose will be deleted or anonymised.

10. Security and Confidentiality

Baerz takes appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration and unlawful disclosure.

These measures may include:

  • Restricted access to personal information.
  • Authentication and access controls.
  • Appropriate security measures for our Website, email and CRM systems.
  • Confidentiality requirements for authorised personnel and service providers.
  • Appropriate technical maintenance and security monitoring.

Access to personal information is limited to authorised persons with a legitimate need to process it.

Although no digital system can guarantee absolute security, Baerz takes appropriate measures to protect the information under its responsibility.

Where a personal data breach occurs, we will assess and fulfil our notification obligations under applicable law.

11. Cookies and Website Analytics

Baerz.com uses functional cookies and limited analytical technologies.

We do not use advertising tracking cookies, behavioural remarketing cookies or third-party advertising profiling cookies.

Functional cookies support necessary Website features, security and user preferences.

Limited analytical technologies help us understand general Website usage and improve performance.

Google Analytics is used for this limited statistical purpose.

Analytics is intended to operate without advertising personalisation, remarketing or unnecessary collection of identifying information.

Functional cookies may be used without prior consent where permitted by law.

Limited analytical cookies may be used without prior consent only where their impact on users' privacy is negligible or limited and all applicable legal conditions are satisfied.

Any cookies or comparable technologies requiring prior consent will only be used after valid consent has been obtained.

Further information is available in our separate Cookie Policy.

12. Marketing Communications

Baerz communicates with users in connection with enquiries and services they have requested.

Separate newsletters and promotional communications are sent only where permitted by applicable law.

Where prior consent is required, it will be obtained before such communications are sent.

Users may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time through the unsubscribe facility or by contacting info@baerz.com.

Unsubscribing from marketing communications does not prevent Baerz from responding to existing enquiries or processing information for another lawful purpose.

We do not sell or disclose subscriber information to unrelated third parties for their independent marketing activities.

13. Your Rights Under the GDPR

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you have the right to:

  • Access: request information about personal data processed concerning you.
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: request deletion of personal information where the legal requirements are satisfied.
  • Restriction: request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability: receive certain information in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format where applicable.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Objection to direct marketing: object to processing for direct marketing purposes at any time.

You also have applicable rights relating to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Baerz does not use ordinary property enquiry information to make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects concerning users.

Requests concerning your privacy rights may be submitted to:

info@baerz.com

We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity.

We will respond within the periods required by the GDPR, generally within one month, subject to any lawful extension.

14. Questions and Complaints

If you have questions or concerns about how your personal information is handled, please contact us at info@baerz.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

In the Netherlands, the supervisory authority is:

Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens

Website: www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl

You may also contact another competent European data protection authority where applicable.

15. External Websites

Baerz.com may contain links to websites operated by independent professionals or other third parties.

These websites may have their own privacy policies and data protection practices.

Baerz does not control the independent processing activities of external website operators.

We encourage users to review the relevant privacy information before submitting personal information to such websites.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Baerz may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legislation, technology, operational procedures or data processing activities.

The current version will be published on the Website with its effective date.

Where required by law, we will provide appropriate additional information or seek consent before implementing relevant changes.

17. Contact

For questions concerning this Privacy Policy, your personal information or the exercise of your data protection rights, please contact:
