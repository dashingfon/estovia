Buying abroad?
 
Avoid common pitfalls.
Before you continue your property search; see what every buyer learns too late.
Every year; we see international buyers fall into the same traps. They spend weeks. They waste far too much time chasing duplicate listings; only to discover the properties are outdated or no longer available. They are registered as “clients” with agencies they never agreed to. Without realizing it; they end up listed as “registered clients” with multiple agencies. And too often; they rely on seller’s brokers who claim to serve both sides; a setup that rarely protects the buyer’s interests.
 
 
These are avoidable missteps; but only if you know where to look. In this short video; we share the three most common pitfalls that turn a dream search into a frustrating experience; and we show you how to approach the international market with clarity; confidence and the right support.
