Why public listings can
undermine value

For owners of exceptional properties, a sale is never just a transaction. It is about privacy, control, and reaching the right buyer under the right conditions. Public listings, price histories, and high visibility can invite unwanted attention, encourage speculation, and weaken negotiating leverage.
With Estoria & Co, your home is never posted on public portals. We manage a private sale process created for high-end real estate, introducing your property to serious, pre-qualified buyers only. The objective is simple, preserve discretion, protect value, and deliver a result that reflects the stature of your home.

How we structure a
global off-market sale

Your property is presented through a cultivated circle of trusted buyers’ agents, family offices, and high‑net‑worth clients. With on‑the‑ground presence in more than twenty countries, and long‑standing relationships across Europe and beyond, we place your home in front of the right people without entering the public domain. Every introduction is deliberate, every conversation discreet, and every step aligned with your objectives.

Why selling Off-Market
protects the value of your
Home

Listing your property publicly doesn't always translate to better outcomes, especially when your home sits within the high-end segment. The longer a luxury residence remains listed across mainstream platforms, the more it risks dilution of price, positioning, and perception.
Off-market sales offer an alternative. Currently, nearly 48% of Europe's luxury transactions are being completed privately, reflecting a clear shift toward greater selectivity and more considered presentation. More sellers are choosing a route that presents their property to a pre-qualified audience with intent to act, not just browse.

For sellers, this means fewer delays, less friction, and a sale process that protects the worth of their home while streamlining its path to the right buyer.

From first conversation
to closing

A senior advisor oversees the process end to end. That includes discreet valuation guidance, tailored positioning, buyer qualification, viewings with appropriate protocol, negotiation management, and coordination through due diligence to completion. You remain fully informed and in control while we handle the work that requires time, experience, and international reach.

With Estoria & Co, selling off-market gives you discretion, control, and access to buyers capable of recognizing and securing the true value of your home.

FAQ

Selling off-market means your property is not listed on public portals like MLS or real estate websites. Instead, it is marketed privately through trusted agents and direct introductions to qualified buyers.

Estoria & Co leverages an extensive global network of trusted buyers' agents, family offices, and high-net-worth individuals across more than twenty countries. Each introduction is curated to match the specific profile of your property, ensuring every prospect is both qualified and genuinely interested.

Luxury homeowners choose off-market sales to protect their privacy, maintain price integrity, and reach only the most serious buyers. Public listings can attract unwanted attention, invite speculative offers, and leave a visible price history that weakens negotiating power.

Our fee structure is discussed transparently during your initial consultation. We work on a professional commission basis aligned with the complexity and value of the sale. There are no hidden charges, and all terms are agreed in advance.
